Let's talk I'm a supplier

What a LivePart looks like

Simple on top. Everything else one tap away.

  1. Send one fileA STEP file and a one-line note. That's it.
  2. Get a LivePartThe drawing is read, risks are flagged and similar past parts are found.
  3. Quote, make, get paidOne button shows the next step. Questions, revisions and payment stay on the same record.

Today

Sending out a part means emails, calls, lookups, and no memory.

Effort

Changes

A Rev B gets re-sent to everyone, and nobody is sure who has the latest drawing.

Outcomes

Records

Who it's for

Built for buyers, suppliers, and the AI and software they use.

Buyers

  • One record per part. Questions, revisions, status and payment stay together.
  • Keep your suppliers. Send to the partners you already trust, or find new US shops that fit.
  • Every job in one view. Partner shops and your own cells, side by side.

Suppliers

  • Nothing new to adopt. Open the link from email and reply there, or with your own PDF.
  • Clear, complete requests. Fewer calls chasing specs, less guessing priced into quotes.
  • A fair process. A small invited group, a decision date, and everyone hears the outcome.

AI and software

  • Agents can buy parts. They send a file and a note; a person approves before anything goes out.
  • Partner software connects. ERP, quoting and design tools work from the same record.
  • Suppliers still get a normal email. Nothing changes on their side.
